# ⌨️ Presentation Keyboard Shortcuts & Cheatsheet

This presentation is designed for clean, distraction-free conference talks with no on-screen UI clutter.

---

## Navigation & Controls

| Shortcut | Action | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`→`** / **`Space`** / **`Enter`** / **`PageDown`** | **Next slide** | Advances with smooth forward motion |
| **`←`** / **`Backspace`** / **`PageUp`** | **Previous slide** | Steps back with reverse motion |
| **`F`** | **Toggle Fullscreen** | Blends the black outer bezel into the projector |
| **`O`** or **`Esc`** | **Slide Navigator** | Opens thumbnail/overview jump drawer |
| **`Home`** | **Jump to Start** | Goes directly to Slide 01 |
| **`End`** | **Jump to End** | Goes directly to the closing slide |

---

## 🖥️ Hidden Dock Toolbar

- **To reveal the toolbar:** Simply move your mouse cursor to the **bottom edge** of the screen.
- The macOS-style dock will smoothly glide up with:
  - Slide counter
  - Previous / Next buttons
  - Overview grid trigger
  - Fullscreen toggle
- Moving the mouse away will smoothly hide the dock again.

## 🎨 Frame Design Details

- **Aspect Ratio:** Strict 16:9 widescreen canvas dynamically optimized for 1080p, 2K, and 4K displays.
- **MacBook Bezel:** Deep pure black surround (`#000000`) with high-precision Apple-style rounded corners (`rounded-[36px]`).
- **Projector Optimized:** Crisp off-white interior (`#FAFAFB`) ensuring readability under any lighting.
